What Can AI Do?

with the progression in the complexity of neural networks we have seen vast changes in its capabilities. AI now has the ability to understand complex systems that make up programs and devices. This of course leads to a major problem in cybersecurity.

Open AI hacks Hugging face

Open AI was doing internal testing on their new agent to test it's capabilities. they were trying to see how fast it could solve a problem. their agent was placed in a sandbox(a defined space with a limit on given information). The AI was given insturctions to solve the problem and the AI unknowingly to OpenAI fully autonoously broke out of the sandbox and hacked hugging face thinking the answer would be there.

Anthropic Hacked 3 Companies

following the disclosure of the Open AI/huggingFace incident Anthorpic questioned if the same could happen or has happened to them and of course, yes it has. they found 3 instances where their agent had hacked a company to find information.

AI responbilitiy

the problem with these instances is the responsiblity, Open AI didnt mean to hack hugging face and neither did Anthropic mean to hack 3 companies but it happened. The speed at which the AI industry is growing has caused many problems in many different ways. AI is too powerful for us to understand and control and I think that the capabilities of AI cannot be underlooked and given these instances and the power AI has we can see how this becomes a problem when, for example, a dangerous hacker uses the AI to intentionally hack a system.
