How Does AI Affect the Cybersecurity industry?

given the capabilities of AI it has become the standard to use AI in the cybersecurity industry. As you could imagine, both Black Hat(dangerous) and white hat(ethical) hackers have to use AI because they have to keep up with eachother and this has caused a major gap in cybersecuirity humans are starting to become less critical for the industry given the fact that AI is more efficient in finding problems.

There is light at the end of the tunnel though, AI has still not progressed to a point where the quality of the code they right is sufficient so even if AI used in the cybersecuirty industry it is still important to have educated and responsible people writing the code and ensuring the safety of a company or information.
